## Script

Caitlin Clark's range gets most of the attention, but the harder part to defend is how early she recognizes an advantage. A defender can make the technically correct choice, only to realize Caitlin has already used it against her.

She waits until both players collapse, which leaves Sophie Cunningham filling the space behind the play. By slowing down for just a moment, Caitlin turns the defense's correct instinct into an open trailer three.

And this is where her range makes every other read more difficult. On this possession, Portland is matched in transition, but the defender picks Caitlin up at a distance that would be completely reasonable against almost any other guard. The help is still below the action, the defender believes she has room to recover, and Caitlin recognizes that the space is already hers. She takes the pull-up from thirty-one feet before Portland can change the coverage.

These plays look different, but the advantage is the same. One defender shifts her weight, two defenders collapse toward the ball, and another gives up ordinary pickup space. Caitlin does not wait for the defense to make an obvious mistake. She makes the defense commit first, then attacks the decision it thought was safe.
