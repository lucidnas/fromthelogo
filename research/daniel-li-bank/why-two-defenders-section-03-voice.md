# Why Defenses Have to Guard Caitlin Clark Twice — Section 3

## Script

But sending help at the first action does not end the possession. Against Connecticut, Clark rejects the spread pick-and-roll and attacks the open side. The low defender rotates toward her drive. That defender is not making a mistake. She is preventing Clark from walking to the rim.

The problem is what happens next. As the low defender steps toward Clark, Hull's route opens behind her. Clark delivers the ball into that space, and Hull uses the reverse side of the rim before the rotation can recover. Connecticut solved the drive and conceded the cut in the same movement.

This is where Clark's range and her passing become one skill. The range pulls the first line higher. The drive moves the second line. Then Clark reads the last defender who can still protect the basket. A defense can survive one correct rotation. Clark keeps asking it to make another one.
