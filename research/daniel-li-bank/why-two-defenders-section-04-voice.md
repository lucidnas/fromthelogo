# Why Defenses Have to Guard Caitlin Clark Twice — Section 4

## Script

Portland gives us the cleanest picture of the original problem. Clark advances on the left side in transition. The nearest defender retreats toward the arc, which is a normal place to meet most ball handlers. Clark gathers from nearly twenty-nine feet and turns that ordinary pickup space into a three.

The defender is not ignoring Clark. She is retreating into the place where the defense would usually become organized. Clark simply begins the shot before that organization is complete. Now imagine moving that pickup point several feet higher on every possession. There is more floor behind the on-ball defender, and less time for everybody else to cover it.

That is why Clark does not need to shoot from this distance every time. The possibility changes where the possession starts. Once the defense meets her earlier, Indiana can attack the extra space before the back line is ready.
