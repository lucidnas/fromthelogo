# Awful Coaching Script Pattern Notes

Source transcript: `/Users/abdul/transcripts/awful-coaching/transcribed/caitlin-clark-too-good-wnba.txt`

Video reviewed: `CAITLIN CLARK is literally too good for the WNBA`

## Core Observation

The script is clip-first, not essay-first. The narration almost never leaves the visible play. Each beat starts by telling the viewer where to look, names the defensive mistake, explains the advantage Caitlin Clark creates, then lands on the payoff.

The title claim is proven through a stack of possessions instead of a broader story arc.

## Structure

1. One-sentence verdict.
2. Immediate first play.
3. Defensive mistake or coverage error.
4. Clark read.
5. Payoff.
6. Quick reaction line.
7. Next play.

There is no long intro, no standings context, no recap framing, and almost no macro narrative. The script trusts the plays.

## Beat Formula

```text
Look/freeze here.
The defender/coverage is late, leaning, stuck, backpedaling, or out of position.
Caitlin sees it before the defense can fix it.
That creates the angle/window/advantage.
Payoff: three, layup, and-one, no-look pass, easy catch.
Reaction: that is ridiculous / that is the punishment / that is why she bends the game.
```

## What To Borrow

- Start with a verdict, then immediately prove it.
- Build the video from 8-12 visible plays.
- Let the defender or coverage mistake be the antagonist.
- Use freeze frames only when the VO points to a specific visible thing.
- Every freeze should answer: what did Clark see before everyone else?
- Every replay should reveal a new layer, not just repeat the highlight.
- Keep each play in cause-effect order.

## What To Avoid

- Do not copy the exact repeated phrasing.
- Do not use filler screen directions when the edit is not actually showing the detail.
- Do not make the VO broader than the clip pool.
- Do not say tactical claims that cannot be validated with official PBP or clear footage.
- Do not let freeze frames be random midpoint stills.

## FTL Adaptation

FTL should keep the same clip-led spine but make the tone more celebratory:

```text
This is the mistake.
This is what Caitlin sees.
This is how she punishes it.
And this is why the box score still undersells the possession.
```

For a 3-5 minute FTL game video, use 6-9 verified Clark plays. Each play gets one compact paragraph and one or two deliberate freeze frames. The script should be written after the selected play manifest is validated against official PBP.

## New Production Rule

Do not write the full VO until the play manifest exists.

Each script paragraph must map to one row:

```text
clipNumber
sourcePath/sourceIn/sourceOut
official actionNumber
official clock/score/result
visible Clark action
what the play proves
approved overlay
approved freeze frame source times
```

If the row does not exist, the VO beat should not exist.
