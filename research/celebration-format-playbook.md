## Spine — How Every Winner Is Structured

### Game footage update: clip-led first

For game-based Celebration videos, use `research/clip-led-clark-celebration-playbook.md` as the operating spine.

The video is not a recap of whether Indiana won or lost. It is a positive breakdown of Caitlin Clark's best plays. If Clark performs great, the video is viable. The structure is footage first: play on screen → defensive mistake → Clark read → Clark punishment → reaction/payoff → next play.

This lets FTL cover wins, strong losses, archived games, and offseason compilations without depending on the final score.

### 1. Cold open with a clip line, not a thesis
Game-call audio, a quote, an ad line. Never "Today we're talking about…"

Examples:
- GENIUS opens with: "I was once that little Iowa girl who was inspired to dream big"
- AWARD opens with: broadcast call → "did she just do that award"
- SPECTACULAR opens with: shot+pass+ball-handle teaser stitched together
- ART opens with: "from snatching people's ankles… Caitlin Clark one thing she got is handles"

### 2. Compilation/listicle spine
The body is 6–10 specific plays/ads/moments. Each beat = one clip + one reaction line. Not narrative — inventory with attitude.

### 3. Game audio + reaction quips
Script is built around real broadcast audio, not over it. The narrator reacts to what we just heard.

### 4. One pop-culture comparison per video
Kobe, MJ, Steph, Kyrie, LeBron, Luka, Jokić. The comparison anchors the awe.

### 5. Reaction quips that punch up
Heavy use of similes — never serious.

Examples from the channel:
- "That girl needs to activate her GPS"
- "Dancing With the Stars without his consent"
- "Like a reliable Door Dash driver"
- "Cotton candy type of pass — just too sweet"
- "One of Carrington's eyelashes popped out and fell on the ground"

### 6. No documentary tone
No "the question is," no "let's set the stage." Pure fan-energy commentary on visible footage.

### 7. Length: 1,200–1,400 words
~8 minutes at narration pace. AWARD (1,206) and GENIUS (1,470) bookend the range.

## Title Format Library

The "THIS Caitlin Clark ___ is ___" pattern. Always include "Caitlin Clark." The yellow word is the hook word that lands on the thumbnail.

### Yellow word library
GENIUS, SPECTACULAR, UNREAL, INSANE, AWARD, DIFFERENT, ART, NEXT LEVEL, ELITE, MASTERFUL, NUTS, RIDICULOUS, COLD

### Title patterns that work
- This Caitlin Clark [Thing] is [Yellow Word]
- This Caitlin Clark [Thing] Deserves An AWARD
- Caitlin Clark [Things] Are PURE [Yellow Word]
- This Caitlin Clark [Thing] is [Famous Athlete] Level

### Title patterns that underperform
- "The Secret Detail in…" — too narrow, no yellow word, framed as detective story (3K views)
- "The X You Have To See" — no yellow word (38K views)
- Any title without "Caitlin Clark" upfront

## Thumbnail (Format D — channel standard)

Clark's face fills left 60% — smiling, laughing, joyful. Dark near-black bokeh background. The yellow word fills the right 35–40% in heavy condensed sans-serif (#FFE84D).

Pick the expression to match the yellow word:
- GENIUS / NEXT LEVEL → wide smile, mid-laugh, pure delight
- AWARD / SPECTACULAR / UNREAL → confident smile, eyes lit up
- ELITE / MASTERFUL → calm, knowing, slight smirk
- ART → joyful, head tilt

## What Goes In A Celebration Video — Source Material

A celebration video must be built around at least one of:

1. **Specific plays from the last 7–14 days** — broadcast audio available, clean clips findable
2. **A new commercial / ad campaign** — full ad available on YouTube
3. **A signature stat-line moment** — e.g., 32 pts on 7 threes vs. Liberty
4. **A categorized compilation** — "all her behind-the-back moves this season," "every logo three"
5. **A side-by-side comparison** — CC plays paired with NBA legend plays

If none of those exist for a given week — don't force a celebration. Wait or pivot to opportunistic refs/fines/game recap (only if a real artifact exists).

## The Anti-Patterns — What Got Sub-1K Views

The two newest videos before this playbook (RESPECTS and BACKFIRED, 490 + 698 views) ignored the spine:

- Cold opens were thesis statements, not clip lines
- Body was narrative arc, not compilation
- No game audio woven through
- No pop-culture comparison
- Documentary tone instead of fan-quip tone
- Yellow words (RESPECTS, BACKFIRED) were villain-frame words, not awe words

The format is forgiving when the spine is right and unforgiving when it isn't. Stick to the spine.
