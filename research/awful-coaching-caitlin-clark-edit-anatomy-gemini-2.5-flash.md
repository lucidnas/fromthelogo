# Awful Coaching Edit Anatomy

## One-Sentence Formula
This video dissects individual basketball plays using continuous, analytical voiceover synchronized with frequent freeze frames, circles, arrows, and slow-motion replays to highlight specific player actions and defensive breakdowns, creating a rapid-fire, instructional breakdown of "awful coaching" moments.

## Global Editing Rules
*   **Clip Length & Pacing:** The video features rapid cuts, averaging 2-5 seconds per distinct visual state (live, freeze, replay). This fast pacing keeps the viewer engaged, constantly presenting new visual information.
*   **Visual Rhythm:** Each play typically starts with a brief live-action segment, transitions to a freeze frame for initial analysis, then uses slow-motion replay with overlays to illustrate the breakdown, and finally shows the live-action payoff.
*   **VO Density:** The voiceover is almost continuous, providing a constant stream of analysis and direction. There are very few moments of silence, ensuring the viewer's attention is always guided.
*   **Replay/Freeze Usage:** Freeze frames are heavily utilized (appearing every 5-10 seconds on average) to pinpoint specific moments of action or inaction. Replays are often shown in slow-motion to allow for detailed analysis.
*   **Screen Direction:** The screen is highly directed using visual overlays. Circles and arrows are almost always present during freeze frames and slow-motion segments to explicitly highlight the player or movement being discussed.
*   **Scoreboard/Clock:** The scoreboard and game clock are consistently visible, providing context but not actively used in the analysis unless directly relevant to a specific play's timing.
