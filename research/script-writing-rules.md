# FTL Script Writing Rules

**Status:** Canonical. These rules govern every FTL script — News Short, Celebration, Courtroom Drama, Documentary. No exceptions.

Last updated: 2026-05-05

---

## Core Objective

Create emotionally engaging stories through excellent storytelling that hooks viewers from the start and leaves them satisfied at the end. Inform, entertain, and **always exceed viewers' expectations** from the moment they start watching.

---

## Core Principles

### Hook Is Everything
- Grab attention within the **first 5–10 seconds** (not 30).
- Use questions, shocking statements, or mysteries to spark curiosity.
- Confirm the click. Over-deliver immediately.

### Emotional Shifts
- Alternate between tension, excitement, humor, and shock.
- Constant emotional changes maintain engagement.

### Show, Don't Tell
- Provide as much **visual material** as possible. Visuals are central to the storytelling.
- Writing acts as a guide for what's being shown — not a replacement for it.
- If something can be shown instead of said, **prioritize the visual**.

### Strong Ending
- End on a high note by maintaining or exceeding the quality of the video.
- Leave a powerful takeaway or memorable final impression.

### Watch Sessions
- Encourage viewing multiple videos by linking content effectively (end screens connect similar themes).
- End screens have **ONE purpose only**. Focus on a single video or action. Avoid multiple options.

### CTAs (Call-to-Actions)
- One clear CTA per script.
- Prefer **visual CTAs** over text or VO.

### Overdeliver
- Deliver more value than promised in the title and thumbnail.
- Conclude with a powerful takeaway or unexpected twist.

### Audience Engagement
- Ask questions that spark curiosity and make viewers want answers from the video.
- Tease upcoming sections with mini-hooks and open loops.

## Core Script Structure

### 1. Hook
**Objective:** Capture attention. Confirm the click.
- Provocative question or statement.
- Mysterious yet relevant.
- Open one loop that pays off later in the video.

### 2. Contextual Overview
**Objective:** Set the stage.
- Key moments in the subject's history.
- Relevant accomplishments or turning points.
- Mini-hook previewing upcoming twists.

### 3. Build-Up
**Objective:** Create suspense leading to the main event.
- Key conflicts or decisions.
- Speculation or rumors.
- More open loops.

### 4. Main Event
**Objective:** Dive into the heart of the story.
- Pivotal moments or decisions.
- Immediate reactions and consequences.
- Rhetorical questions to draw viewers in.

### 5. Immediate Aftermath
**Objective:** Analyze short-term impact.
- Criticism, controversy, notable reactions.
- Mini-hook hinting at long-term implications.

### 6. Long-Term Repercussions
**Objective:** Reflect on broader implications and legacy.
- Ongoing criticism, career moves.
- **Resolve the open loops** introduced earlier.

### 7. Outro
**Objective:** Leave viewers intrigued and ready for the next video.
- Summarize key takeaways.
- Tease related content.
- One direct CTA — single, specific viewer action.

> Note: Short News (90s–3min) compresses these — Hook → Build-Up → Main Event → Outro. Open loops still required even in short form.

## Style Guide

### Narrative Perspective
- **Default to first-person singular ("I")** for direct, personal takes. Builds connection.
- Use "we" sparingly — only for shared experiences ("We all remember when…").

### Tone & Language
- **Conversational yet authoritative.** Engaging and informative. Not casual, not formal.
- **Confident and direct.** Opinions delivered with conviction.
- **Informal.** A discussion with a basketball-knowledgeable friend, not a robotic fact-driven narrative.

### Humor & Emotion
- Subtle, smart humor where it fits naturally. Never forced.
- Emotional variety: excitement, surprise, tension.

### Cursing
- Minimal. A mild curse can land emotionally but excessive swearing alienates the audience.

### Sentence Structure & Pacing
- Short, punchy sentences. Every sentence moves the story forward.
- Vary sentence length to maintain rhythm and prevent monotony.

### Visual Storytelling Priority
- **Minimal narration when possible.** Let visuals do the heavy lifting.
- Voiceover enhances what is seen.
- Descriptive but not excessive.

---

## Transitions

### Open Loops
Essential for end-to-end engagement. Open a question or topic that resolves later in the script. Boosts retention and AVD.

### Mini-Hooks
Critical before chapter cards or pauses within the video. Faster than open loops. Each one teases the upcoming section.

### CTAs

**Hidden CTAs:** Subtly encourage viewers to interact without directly asking. Indirectly ask for opinions/thoughts on a specific part of the video — creates engagement seamlessly.

**Direct CTAs:** Clearly ask one specific action — usually subscribe.
- **Never** "Like, Comment, and Subscribe" — viewers do none of the three.
- One single, straightforward request increases follow-through.

## How This Applies to Each FTL Format

| Format | Hook timing | Length | Open loops | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **News Short** | 5–8s | 90s–3min, 350–700 words | 1–2 mini-hooks | One visual CTA + one direct outro CTA |
| **Celebration** | 5–10s | 4–6 min, 1,200–1,400 words | 2–3 open loops, 2 mini-hooks | One direct CTA |
| **Courtroom Drama** | 5–10s | 5–8 min, 1,200–1,400 words | 3 open loops, 3 mini-hooks | One direct CTA |
| **Documentary** | 5–10s | 8–10 min, 1,500–2,000 words | 4+ open loops, 4 mini-hooks | One direct CTA |

---

## What This Rule Set Replaces / Refines

- **Replaces** the AGENTS.md "30-second hook" guidance — new standard is 5–10 seconds.
- **Adds** mandatory open loops + mini-hooks at every chapter break.
- **Locks** one CTA per script (was previously ambiguous).
- **Locks** end-screen single-purpose rule.
- **Refines** Show-Don't-Tell as the visual priority — narration is secondary to visuals, not the other way around.

---

## Pre-flight checklist (run before VO)

1. ✅ Hook lands in first 5–10 seconds.
2. ✅ At least one open loop opened in the first 30s.
3. ✅ Every chapter break has a mini-hook forward.
4. ✅ All open loops resolved before outro.
5. ✅ Sentence length varies (short punches + medium beats).
6. ✅ One single direct CTA, not "like comment subscribe".
7. ✅ End screen connects to ONE specific next video.
8. ✅ Voice = first-person ("I") for opinions, "we" only for shared moments.
9. ✅ Roast tool sentiment = strong or fire.
