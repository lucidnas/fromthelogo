## Core Facts & Receipts

- Indiana beat the Connecticut Sun 85-75 on June 13, 2026. _(CBS/AP)_
- Caitlin Clark had 25 points on 10-of-17 shooting (5-of-10 from three). _(SI takeaways)_
- It was Clark's second straight 25-point game — her first back-to-back 25s since her rookie season. _(SI takeaways)_
- Clark continues to lead the WNBA in assists; SI described her as "playing at an MVP level." _(SI takeaways)_
- Clark was assessed a personal technical foul in the game; afterward she said of it, verbatim: "I deserved it, but it was worth it." _(SI)_ — NOTE: SI and the official WNBA play-by-play conflict on the timing/trigger of this tech (SI describes a 3rd-quarter air-punch; the official feed logs her technical at 0:22 of the 4th). Because of that conflict, the script must NOT state the quarter or the exact trigger as fact — just that she got a technical and said this.
- **The "ridiculous" call was a DELAY-OF-GAME TEAM TECHNICAL (not a personal foul on Clark), late in the fourth quarter:** after a Sun inbound, the loose ball went off Clark's foot (per her account, off the scorer's table) as she pursued it, and referee Tyler Mirkovich called delay of game. _(SI)_
- Clark on that one — verbatim: "The delay of game made no sense. It felt like Tyler wanted to insert himself into the game, and that was ridiculous. So, not a point in the game he needed to call that. I went and got the ball, went off my foot off the scorer's table, and he gave us a delay of game which is a team technical foul. You can ask Tyler, I don't agree with that." _(SI)_
- Sophie Cunningham returned from a one-game absence and scored Indiana's final 11 points (including clutch threes) to seal it. _(SI / B-R)_
- The Fever won their third straight, controlled the glass, and improved to 8-5 (4-1 in Commissioner's Cup play). _(CBS/AP, SI)_
