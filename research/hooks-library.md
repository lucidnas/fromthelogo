# FTL Hooks Library

Pulled from the audience-research transcripts (`~/transcripts/audience-research/`) of every FTL or FTL-adjacent video that crossed 50K views. Use this as the source of truth for opening any new script. **Do not invent a new hook.** Pick a template, slot in today's facts.

Last updated: 2026-04-28.

## Template 1 — Cold Play-by-Play (broadcast audio first)

The narrator starts mid-action. The first sentence is the play itself in present tense, almost transcribed from the broadcast call.

| Video | Views | Opening line |
|---|---|---|
| Demolished | 2M | "so here's Aaliyah Boston stealing the ball and finding Caitlin Clark on the break but as soon as she's about to build up ahead of steam Diamond the Shields just bulldozed her" |
| Took Down Top Seed | 634K | "so here's DiJonai Carrington hounding Caitlin Clark and right before she could even get the chance to defend her Caitlin just floored her by knocking her off to her own teammate before drilling a wide-open three-pointer" |
| Long Passes | 529K | "90 seconds left to play here in the first quarter. Looking for Reese, Bendlay tips it away and off and running. Caitlin Clark rips a pass. Thread the needle." |
| Mesmerizing Assists | 329K | "quickly up the floor is Mitchell. Beautiful find from Clark. Bark bounces. Stoy able to finish three. Look at the velocity on this pass, Ryan." |
| Skill SPECTACULAR | 204K | "from making big time buckets — Clark has space she's going to let it fly and she's going to connect — to dishing out eye-popping assists" |
| Play AWARD | 204K | "[broadcast call] over ratio in her career last season Clark gets away with a little push call away buries it whoa whoa whoa whoa hold on a second let's watch that clip one more time" |

**When to use:** Celebration videos built around a play, a skill, a pass, a shot, a dribble. **Default for the celebration format.** The hook is the broadcast doing your work for you.

**Formula:** `[broadcast call audio] + "hold on, run that back" or "wait what just happened" + the case being made today`

## Template 2 — "Y'all I Can't Believe This" Outrage

Direct address. Fan-to-fan. The narrator is upset and recruiting the viewer to be upset too. Used for ref/fine/villain videos.

| Video | Views | Opening line |
|---|---|---|
| Flagrant Backfired | 211K | "Y'all, are you kidding me right now? I know you've all watched this play 100 times already, but I still can't believe Caitlin Clark got a flagrant foul on Angel Reese for this." |
| Tech Backfired | 220K | "Y'all, I still can't believe this happened. Caitlin Clark got hit with the softest, most ridiculous technical foul I've ever seen in my life." |

**When to use:** Refs/fines/villain reactions only. Never for celebrations.

**Formula:** `"Y'all" + "I can't believe" + the specific bad call/decision + the specific ridiculous detail`

## Template 3 — Clark's Own Voice First

Open with Caitlin Clark herself speaking. Her quote, her interview, her commercial. The narrator doesn't appear until 20+ seconds in.

| Video | Views | Opening line |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial GENIUS | 217K | "I was once that little Iowa girl who was inspired to dream big, real big. And that dream was more than women's basketball." |

**When to use:** Commercial breakdowns. Quote-driven angles (e.g. "I'm the best transition player in the league"). Ad campaigns where Clark is the talent.

**Formula:** `[Clark's own voice/quote, full sentence, no narrator] + cut to narrator with "that was Caitlin Clark on [date/context]"`

## Template 4 — "Every Single Time" Receipt-Stack

Build a pattern in the first 20 seconds. Multiple instances of the same wrong. Then drop the response moment.

| Video | Views | Opening line |
|---|---|---|
| Angel Reese Refused | 69K | "Multiple times in this Team USA game, Angel Reese had the rebound, Caitlin Clark was open, and the outlet was right there. And every single time, the ball still didn't get to Caitlin." |

**When to use:** Villain/snub videos where the case is built across multiple plays. Especially good when there's a pattern of disrespect to document.

**Formula:** `"Multiple times" + the specific behavior + "every single time" + the specific exclusion + then Caitlin's response moment`

## Template 5 — External Validator Quote

Open with somebody else (analyst, ex-WNBA player, Charles Barkley, podcast guest) hyping Clark or defending her. Borrow their authority.

| Video | Views | Opening line |
|---|---|---|
| Shoots SPECTACULAR | 684K | "you know when I look at somebody like Caitlin Clark I think about this just shift in the impact of basketball — ladies and gentlemen I love Caitlin Clark I'm the one that's been calling her the Steph Curry of women's basketball" |
| Sophie Snapped | 83K | "And it literally like pisses me off when people are like, 'She's not the face of the league.' Who would be?" |

**When to use:** Anchor a celebration to a respected voice's stamp. Or anchor a defense to someone else's frustration.

**Formula:** `[external person's audio/quote] + "that was [name]" + "and they're right because…"`

**Formula:** `[full villain quote runs] + "that was [name]" + the specific damage/context`

## Template 7 — Set Up The Slight, Then Cue The Response

Two-beat opener: scene-set the disrespect first, then bring Clark/Fever in.

| Video | Views | Opening line |
|---|---|---|
| Revenge Bullies | 1M | "'I get two shits' — that's how Cheryl Reeve welcomed Caitlin Clark and the Indiana Fever in Minnesota. Now to add more fuel to the fire, fans caught Reeve wearing a shirt…" |
| Humbled Taurasi | 584K | "after narrowly escaping France with a one-point win in the gold medal round everyone was excited to see members of Team USA go back and play in the WNBA — having three gold medalists on their lineup the Phoenix Mercury posted a hype video on TikTok" |
| Sophie Backfired | 343K | "Sophie Cunningham just got fined. And what happened next? She went viral like never before. The WNBA hit her with a $400 slap on the wrist. But the internet turned her into an icon." |

**When to use:** Karma/villain-response videos. Set up the bad behavior in 1–2 sentences, then "and then this happened."

**Formula:** `[the slight: quote / shirt / fine / hype video] + "but then" / "and what happened next" + cue Clark's or the Fever's response`

## Picking The Right Template

| If your story is… | Use template |
|---|---|
| A celebration of a play, skill, pass, or moment | 1 (Cold Play-by-Play) |
| A new commercial or Clark quote | 3 (Clark's Own Voice) |
| A pattern of disrespect across multiple incidents | 4 (Every Single Time) |
| A bad call, fine, or league decision | 2 (Y'all I Can't Believe) |
| A celebration anchored to an analyst's praise | 5 (External Validator) |
| A villain saying something dumb on tape | 6 (Coach/Villain Quote First) |
| A revenge / karma / response narrative | 7 (Set Up Slight, Cue Response) |

## Anti-Pattern: What 490–698-View Videos Did

The recent under-1K videos (RESPECTS, BACKFIRED) opened with:
- A thesis statement ("Caitlin Clark just did something the WNBA can't undo")
- A documentary-style scene-set ("It's late April 2026. The world is watching.")
- A direct narrator address with no clip, no quote, no broadcast audio

None of those match a winning template. **If your opening line is a sentence the narrator wrote rather than something a real person said or a real broadcast called, you've already lost the hook.**
